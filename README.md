## 📖 What is GameShelf?

GameShelf is a game tracking application made for people who want a simple way to keep their gaming library organized.

Instead of trying to remember every game you've played, GameShelf gives you a place to keep track of your games and their information.

You can use it to keep an overview of things such as:

* 🎮 Games in your library
* 🏷️ Game genres
* 💻 Platforms
* ⏱️ Playtime
* 📌 Current game status
* 📅 Game information

The idea is simple:

> **Your games, organized in one shelf.**

## Why GameShelf?

Gaming libraries can get messy as you collect more games.

You might have games you've finished, games you're currently playing, games you've stopped playing, and games you haven't even started yet.

GameShelf is designed to make it easier to keep track of all of them in one place.

## What Can You Track?

### Your Games

Keep a collection of the games you own or play.

###  Playtime

Keep track of how much time you've spent playing a game.

### Status

Know where you currently are with each game.

For example:

* Playing
* Completed
* Backlog
* Paused

###  Game Information

Organize your games using information such as genre and platform.
